# Raw microbenchmark results — 2026-09-04

> **Correction (same day, after Astra review):** the original `read_sum` kernel's conditional
> sink let nvcc elide *all* global loads (0 LDG in SASS); the first-pass streaming rows below
> were measuring an empty kernel. `bw_paths.cu` now writes unconditional per-thread output and
> host-verifies checksums. Re-run at 4 GiB **with STAR index build running concurrently**:
> device 207 GB/s, pinned 191, managed 147, plain-malloc 141; random gather 2.6 G/s device /
> 2.3 G/s managed / 16 M/s plain-4K / 9.8 M/s pinned-4K. Conclusions unchanged: unified access
> ≈ device-local for streaming, and the 4 KiB-page cliff is real and ~150×. The `chase.cu`
> "1T latency" column is a 128-thread block, not one chain — treat it as relative only.
> Full re-run on an idle box is Astra test T6, tracked on the Kanban.

Sources: `bench/cuda/bw_paths.cu`, `bench/cuda/chase.cu`, `bench/cuda/chase_thp.cu` (Spark),
`bench/metal/bw_mlx.py` (M4 Pro). Best-of-N reported.

## DGX Spark (GB10, sm_121, CUDA 13.0, driver 580.173.02 open, kernel 6.17 nvidia)

`pageableMemoryAccess=1 concurrentManagedAccess=1 hostNativeAtomic=1`. THP = `madvise` mode.
vLLM (qwen38-flash) was resident (~100 GiB) during the 1/4 GiB runs, stopped for 12/16 GiB runs.

### chase_thp — 4 GiB working set, effect of transparent huge pages

| memory class | Mlk/s @64K T | Mlk/s @1M T |
|---|---|---|
| mmap anon 4K pages (HMM) | 16 | 12 |
| mmap + MADV_HUGEPAGE (HMM, 2 MiB THP) | 2343 | 2323 |
| THP + cudaHostRegister (pinned) | 2315 | 2297 |
| cudaMalloc (device) | 2618 | 2506 |

Note: runs where a buffer was allocated *after* other large buffers sometimes got 4K pages
despite madvise (AnonHugePages delta < buffer) and fell to ~11 Mlk/s; also one
`illegal memory access` on a cudaHostRegister'd THP buffer. Order-dependent — needs a
robust allocator (explicit hugetlbfs or `cudaMallocManaged` + `cudaMemAdvise`) before
relying on THP in the real pipeline.
